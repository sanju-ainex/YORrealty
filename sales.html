The 4-Step Institutional Sales Process

How YOR Realty guarantees total security, speed, and peace of mind during property transactions.

01

Forensic Title Scrutiny

High Court legal advocates thoroughly verify 30-year prior deeds, EC, tax records, revenue sketch, and sub-registrar registers to ensure zero litigation.

02

Physical Inspection & Appraisal

Chartered civil engineers and revenue surveyors conduct boundary verification, structural appraisal, and sub-registrar valuation analysis.

03

Agreement & Escrow Terms

Standardized corporate sale agreements drafted with explicit handover dates, advance terms, and pre-approved bank mortgage syndication.

04

Registration & Keys Handover

Legal counsel physically assists at the Sub-Registrar office for deed registration, tax transfer, mutation, and formal physical handover.
