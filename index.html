Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about property purchasing, NRI Power of Attorney, 30-year deed scrutiny, and registration fees in Kerala.

Our senior legal counsel conducts an unbroken 30-year title lineage check. This includes verifying prior sale deeds, settlement deeds, partition deeds, Patta records, Thandaper extract, revenue tax receipts, non-attachment certificates from civil courts, and encumbrance certificates (EC) from the Sub-Registrar Office. You receive a formal legal scrutiny report before making any financial commitment.

Yes. NRIs can execute a Special Power of Attorney (PoA) attested by the Indian Embassy or Consulate in their country of residence. Once adjudicated by the District Registrar in Kerala, YOR Realty coordinates the entire registration process, tax payments, and title handover seamlessly on your behalf.

In Kerala, stamp duty is generally 8% of the consideration or fair value (whichever is higher), and the registration fee is 2%, totaling approximately 10% of the property value. YOR Realty provides exact, transparent cost sheets with zero hidden surcharges prior to deed drafting.

Yes. Because every listed asset has pre-verified clear title deeds, our properties qualify for priority home loan syndication with leading banks including State Bank of India, Federal Bank, and HDFC at competitive institutional interest rates.
