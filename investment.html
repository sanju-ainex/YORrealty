The 4-Step Investment Governance Framework

How YOR Realty mitigates risk and optimizes investment yield for domestic and international investors.

01

Micro-Market ROI Audit

Rigorous analysis of traffic flow, upcoming government infrastructure projects, sub-registrar transaction trends, and net rental yields.

02

Forensic Title Scrutiny

High Court legal advocates verify 30-year prior deeds, patta, survey boundary sketches, and obtain certified nil-encumbrance reports.

03

Acquisition & Lease Novation

Execution of clean conveyance deed, transfer of corporate tenant security deposits, and novation of rental lease contracts directly to you.

04

Ongoing rent collection, property tax compliance, quarterly inspection audits, and structured exit liquidation after your target holding period.
