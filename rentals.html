Institutional Governance

The 4-Step Rental Governance Framework

How we protect landlords' asset values and provide tenants with a dignified, hassle-free tenancy.

01

Forensic Tenant KYC

Verification of Aadhaar, PAN, corporate employment ID, salary slips, and submission of local police tenant verification forms.

02

Registered Legal Lease

High Court legal counsel prepares water-tight rental deeds with fixed escalation terms, lock-in period, and clear maintenance responsibilities.

03

Photographic Move-In Audit

Timestamped photographic documentation of all electrical appliances, fittings, wall finishes, and plumbing before key handover.

04

Active Custodianship

Monthly rent collection, swift resolution of repair requests, and professional checkout inspection upon tenancy conclusion.
